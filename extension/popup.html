How to measure.

  • Bust or chest. Around the fullest part, tape level. Example: 90.
  • Waist. Around the narrowest part. Example: 72.
  • Hips. Around the widest part. Example: 97.
  • Height. Standing, no shoes. Example: 165.

Use a soft tape, snug but not tight. Between sizes? Size up for a looser fit. Everything stays in this browser.
