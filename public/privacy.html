Privacy

FitForm does not collect, sell or share any data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the extension stores. The measurements you enter (bust or chest, waist, hips, height, and women or men) are saved in your browser only, using local extension storage. They never leave your device.

What it reads. On shopping pages it checks the page's product metadata to decide whether to show the size badge. It does not read your browsing history, page text, forms, or anything you type, and it makes no network requests.

Delete your data. Remove the extension, or clear its storage, and your measurements are gone.

Size results are estimates. Check the brand's own size guide before you buy.
